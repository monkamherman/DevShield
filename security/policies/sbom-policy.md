# Phase 06 SBOM Policy

The final container image must have a non-empty, valid CycloneDX JSON SBOM generated by Syft `1.51.0`. The SBOM must be generated from the image, not merely from source files. Its artifact inventory must contain a BuildKit-provided `sha256` image digest and correlate it with the same image reference used for the SBOM and Trivy evidence.

Missing digest, missing/empty SBOM, invalid JSON/schema, Syft failure or digest mismatch produces `TOOL_FAILURE` (or `SECURITY_FAILURE` for an integrity mismatch). A valid SBOM produces `PASS`. SBOMs and inventories are CI artifacts under ignored `reports/`; they are not committed. SBOM is an observed inventory, not proof of provenance or authenticity. No exceptions are configured.
